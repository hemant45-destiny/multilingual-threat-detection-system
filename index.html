ℹ️ How the Scanner Works
Rule-Based Detection
Checks for URL shorteners, risky TLDs (.xyz .ru .onion), IP-based URLs, phishing keywords in the URL path, and excessive subdomains.
External Verification
Click-through links to VirusTotal (70+ AV engines), PhishTank, URLhaus, Google Safe Browsing, URLScan.io, and OTX AlienVault.
Risk Verdict
Assigns a 0–100 risk score. Below 20 = Likely Safe, 20–49 = Suspicious, 50+ = Malicious based on weighted rule scoring.
